Contoso Electronics Support Policy

Support Tiers:
1. Basic (Free with purchase): 
   - Email support only
   - 48-hour response time
   - Access to online knowledge base and community forums
   - Software updates included

2. Premium ($9.99/month):
   - Phone + email support
   - 4-hour response time during business hours
   - Priority queue for repairs
   - Extended software support
   - Remote diagnostics

3. Enterprise (Custom pricing - contact sales):
   - Dedicated support engineer
   - 1-hour SLA for critical issues
   - On-site support available
   - Custom integration assistance
   - Quarterly business reviews

Warranty Coverage:
- Hardware defects: Full replacement within warranty period
- Software issues: Free updates and patches for 2 years from purchase
- Battery degradation: Replacement if capacity drops below 80% within 1 year
- Accidental damage: NOT covered under standard warranty (see Contoso Care+ insurance)

Return Policy:
- 30 days from purchase date for full refund
- Product must be in original packaging with all accessories
- Opened software and digital downloads are non-refundable
- Restocking fee of 15% applies to opened electronics returned after 14 days
- Defective items can be returned at any time within warranty period

Contact Information:
- Phone: 1-800-CONTOSO (available Mon-Fri, 8AM-8PM EST)
- Email: support@contoso.com
- Chat: Available 24/7 on contoso.com
- Retail locations: Visit contoso.com/stores for nearest location
