Contoso Electronics Troubleshooting Guide

Common Issues and Solutions:

== Contoso Hub ==
Problem: Hub not connecting to Wi-Fi
Solution: 
1. Ensure your router supports 2.4GHz (Hub does not support 5GHz-only networks)
2. Move Hub within 30 feet of router
3. Factory reset: Hold power button for 15 seconds until LED flashes red
4. Re-run setup in the Contoso Home app

Problem: Voice commands not recognized
Solution:
1. Check microphone is not muted (top LED should be green)
2. Speak clearly within 15 feet of the Hub
3. Update firmware via Contoso Home app > Settings > Device Updates

== Contoso Watch Pro ==
Problem: Battery draining quickly
Solution:
1. Disable always-on display (Settings > Display > Always On > Off)
2. Reduce GPS tracking frequency (Settings > Activity > GPS > Battery Saver)
3. Turn off continuous heart rate monitoring if not needed
4. Ensure latest firmware is installed

Problem: Heart rate sensor inaccurate
Solution:
1. Wear watch snugly 1 finger-width above wrist bone
2. Clean sensor with soft cloth
3. Ensure no tattoos or hair blocking sensor
4. Restart watch if readings seem stuck

== Contoso Buds ==
Problem: One earbud not charging
Solution:
1. Clean charging contacts with dry cotton swab
2. Ensure earbud is properly seated in case (should click)
3. Reset: Place both buds in case, hold case button 10 seconds
4. If issue persists, contact support for warranty replacement

Problem: Audio cutting out
Solution:
1. Ensure phone Bluetooth is on and within 30 feet
2. Remove other Bluetooth devices that may interfere
3. Reset Bluetooth pairing: Forget device, re-pair from scratch
4. Update firmware via Contoso Audio app

== General Tips ==
- Always keep firmware updated for best performance
- Register products at contoso.com/register for warranty activation
- Join Contoso Community forums for tips from other users
